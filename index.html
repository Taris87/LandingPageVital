01 — Services

Three ways we help you keep moving

Explore the services and find the right support for your goals.

Kinesiology Taping

Kinesiology tape supports natural motion and can ease pain and muscle spasms. Athletic taping supports a joint and limits motion where an existing pain calls for it.

  • Supports movement
  • Eases pain and spasms
  • Stabilises with athletic taping

Personal Medical Training

One-to-one sessions tailored to you and your goals, for everyday life, after an injury or to stay active.

  • Tailored sessions
  • Increased strength and mobility
  • Improved energy levels

Wellness Massage

Hands-on massage focused on relaxation, stress relief and overall well-being. It is a wellness service and does not treat specific medical conditions.

  • Relaxation
  • Stress relief
  • Overall well-being

02 — Approach

Care that starts with your body

At Vital Movement Center, we are passionate about helping people move with greater confidence, improve mobility, manage discomfort, and maintain an active, healthy lifestyle.

Through personalized care and a hands-on approach, we offer services designed to support your body's individual needs and goals.

  • Working through an injury
  • Managing everyday aches and pains
  • Building strength and mobility
  • Simply wanting to feel your best

Whether you are working through an injury or simply want to feel your best, Vital Movement Center is here to support you. We believe movement is an important part of living a healthy, fulfilling life. Our mission is to help you stay active, keep moving, and enjoy life to the fullest.
